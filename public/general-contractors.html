A Sub You Can Count On

If you're a GC who needs a reliable asphalt subcontractor, you've found one. A1 shows up, does the work right, and doesn't create problems on your jobsite. We know how commercial construction schedules work.

We handle the full scope of exterior pavement work — new asphalt paving, sealcoating, striping, crack filling, concrete curb and gutter, bollards, and ADA compliance. One sub for everything below the building.

We're bonded and insured for commercial work. We provide certificates of insurance in your required format, comply with your safety programs, and attend pre-construction meetings when needed.

A1 partners with general contractors as a reliable subcontractor for all pavement and concrete work. We are licensed, insured, and experienced working on GC-managed projects with tight schedules and strict quality requirements.

Services available for subcontract include asphalt paving, sealcoating, line striping, crack filling, patching, bollard installation, ADA curb cuts, and concrete flatwork. We provide all required insurance certificates, lien waivers, and project documentation.
